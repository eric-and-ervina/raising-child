# The First Three Months — Development, Play & Watching Him Grow

> **For:** Eldric, 0–3 months (26 Oct 2026 → ~26 Jan 2027). The philosophy and 0–18-year plan live in [`../parenting/parenting-guide-raising-a-healthy-grounded-boy.md`](../parenting/parenting-guide-raising-a-healthy-grounded-boy.md) §10 — this file is the practical week-to-week layer underneath it: what he can do, what to do with him, and what is (and isn't) worth worrying about.
> **Rule of the quarter:** milestones are *ranges to observe*, not deadlines to hit or tests to pass. You record what you see, you mention concerns at PD visits ([`../health/pd-visits-and-vaccinations.md`](../health/pd-visits-and-vaccinations.md)), you don't drill a newborn.

---

## What he needs from you at this age (it's short)

1. **Faces and voices** — yours, close up (~20–30 cm is what he sees best). Talk to him while you change, feed, and carry him. Narration is the curriculum.
2. **Tummy time** — the one physical "exercise" that matters now (below).
3. **Response** — when he cries and you come, that's attachment being built (parenting guide §1). You cannot spoil a newborn by responding.
4. **Language in all three** — English, Mandarin, and Indonesian/Bahasa around him naturally, from both parents and grandparents. No formal plan needed yet; exposure and songs count. (A fuller trilingual plan is a future `language-plan.md`.)
5. **Sleep and feeds** — still most of his day. Play lives in the short awake windows; a "good day" at this age is fed, slept, held — anything else is a bonus.

## Tummy time — the progression

Start in **Week 1**, on your chest or a firm floor mat/blanket (the shopping list was right: a folded blanket works at first — the playmat is a 1–2 month purchase).

| Age | Target | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1–2 | A few minutes total/day, in short bouts | Lifts head briefly, turns it side to side. On your chest counts. Always awake + supervised; stop when he's had enough |
| Month 1 | ~15–30 min total/day, spread out | Pushes up on forearms briefly, holds head up for seconds |
| Month 2 | ~30–60 min total/day | Head up ~45°, chest starting to lift, watches a face/toy |
| Month 3 | ~60+ min total/day | Head up ~90° on forearms, swipes at hanging toys, may show early rolling signs (→ swaddle stops — see [`../health/newborn-care.md`](../health/newborn-care.md)) |

Little and often beats one long session he hates. After a feed is the wrong time (spit-up); awake-and-calm before a feed works.

## Month by month — what typically shows up

**Month 1 (now → ~26 Nov).** Sleeps/eats/crying is the whole repertoire, plus: startles, grips a finger placed in his palm, focuses on faces, begins to quiet at your voice. First real **social smiles usually appear ~6 weeks** — earlier "smiles" are mostly reflex (enjoy them anyway). **Your jobs:** survive in shifts, feeds + diaper log, tummy time on chest, PD jaundice + 1-month checks.

**Month 2 (~26 Nov → 26 Dec).** Social smiles on purpose, cooing, tracks a moving face/toy across his view, holds head up longer. **Awake windows lengthen** — this is when black-and-white/high-contrast cards and a simple play gym start earning their space. **2-month PD visit + first big vaccine round ~26 Dec.** Infant care becomes legally possible from 2 months, if that's ever the plan — full foreigner fees, waitlists are real (see paperwork checklist).

**Month 3 (~26 Dec → 26 Jan).** Laughs and richer cooing/babbling back at you, bats at toys, brings hands to mouth, head steady when held upright, may roll tummy→back late in the month. **Ervina's maternity leave ends ~18 Jan** — whatever care arrangement comes next (Eric's leave/flex, grandparents, infant care, helper) needs deciding in December, not January. (Confinement meals end 22 Nov; the household running plan after that is a conversation for late November, logged here when decided.)

## Play & reading — keep it this simple

- **Talk, sing, read aloud** — any book, any language; board books with faces/contrast for looking, your voice for everything else. Five minutes inside an awake window is a full session.
- **High-contrast images** (Month 1–2), then colour and a play gym (Month 2–3). Second-hand is ideal — the prep list's rule stands: buy for the next 2–3 months only, especially in 689 sqft.
- **Carry him around the flat and outside** (pram walks once you're both up to it) — the world is the toy at this age. Faces at the market teach more than any gadget.
- **No screens as entertainment at this age.** Video calls with grandparents in Jakarta are the sensible exception — that's family, not content.

## When to raise it at the PD — not emergencies, just don't sit on them

Mention at the next PD visit (sooner if several stack up): **not** making eye contact / not tracking faces by ~2 months · no social smile by ~2 months · no response to loud sounds or to familiar voices · very stiff or very floppy tone, or a strong one-sided preference (always turning/using one side only) · head control not progressing across Month 2–3 · feeding or growth concerns at any point. Anything acute (fever, breathing, won't wake) is *not* this list — that's [`../health/red-flags-and-emergencies.md`](../health/red-flags-and-emergencies.md), immediately.

Most variations are just variation. The log + the PD's growth chart are how you tell the difference without spiralling at 2 a.m.
