## Back — what to do

**Fever ≥38.0 °C in a baby under 3 months = hospital, now. No waiting for morning.**

**Call 995 for:** trouble breathing, blue/grey lips, seizure, limp or unresponsive, forceful/repeated vomiting, bloody stool, a baby who won't wake to feed.

**Call TMC 6250 2222 / KKH 6225 5554** for urgent advice that isn't a 995 call.

**Choking:** face-down on your forearm, head lower than chest — **5 back blows**, then turn face-up — **5 chest thrusts**. Repeat. Call 995.

**Never shake him.** If you feel overwhelmed, put him safely on his back in the bare cot and step away for five minutes. He is allowed to cry; he is not allowed to be hurt.

**No water, honey, or medicine unless the PD says so.**

## Fill these five today

1. **PD name + phone + after-hours route** — the single most useful line on the card.
2. **Nearest hospital + route** — decide it on a calm day, not during the emergency.
3. **Parents' phone numbers** — write them, don't assume the phone will be unlocked.
4. **G6PD status** — if deficient, every doctor and caregiver needs to know.
5. **Emergency contact** — who to call if neither parent answers.

Once filled: print two copies (wallet + fridge), photograph the front and back, and save the photos in both phones' favourites.
