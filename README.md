# Raising a Child — Personal Notes for Eldric's First Years

Personal planning and reference documents for a Chinese-Indonesian couple in Singapore raising their first child in a **689 sqft rented HDB flat** — written to be usable at 3 a.m. by a first-time father with no experience, not just read once.

**He's here: Eldric Maverick Lim (林凯平), born Mon 26 Oct 2026** at Thomson Medical Centre — vaginal, unmedicated; 3,590 g, 52 cm, head 35 cm. Ervina is recovering (minor tear, 1 stitch); Eric is on confinement-cooking + support duty. The pregnancy-era framing in older files ("week 29 / week 36 / EDD") is historical — where a file and this README disagree about *when we are*, this README wins.

## Start here — the newborn weeks, in order

1. **Tonight / this week:** [`health/newborn-care.md`](health/newborn-care.md) — holding, diapering, cord, bathing, safe sleep, soothing, what's normal, the skin/eye/mouth things that look alarming and aren't, and the visitor/hygiene rules.
2. **When he won't stop crying:** [`health/crying-and-soothing.md`](health/crying-and-soothing.md) — the dedicated playbook: medical screen first, the 9-point sweep, every technique with its failure mode, the 5–11 p.m. peak, colic vs reflux, the pain hunt, the parent-side safe-put-down plan, and a printable fridge card. Read it once before the first bad night; it is built for 3 a.m.
3. **When he cries the second you put him down, and neither of you sleeps:** [`health/night-and-sleep.md`](health/night-and-sleep.md) — the file for the actual failure case: father holds him and he still cries, mother feeds him and he still cries, he falls asleep and screams when he touches the cot. The 7-step lay-down, the five-rung cot ladder (no cry-it-out), the night-feed script, what is and isn't safe when he'll only sleep on a person, the 3 a.m. doze protocol, and your own sleep maths. **Read §0–§4 in daylight; put §10 on the fridge.**
4. **"Is he getting enough — or too much?":** [`health/weight-and-growth.md`](health/weight-and-growth.md) — his pre-filled weight track from 3,590 g with the real call-the-PD numbers, how to weigh at home without losing your mind (once a week, same scale), how to tell intake without a bottle, the 150 ml/kg/day maths, what overfeeding actually looks like, and the oversupply picture. §0 answers it in 30 seconds.
5. **The rules, all on one page:** [`health/dos-and-donts.md`](health/dos-and-donts.md) — the ten that matter most, then do/don't tables for holding, sleep, feeding, cord/skin, visitors and illness, fever, outings and home safety, each with the reason. This is the page to show a grandparent or a helper, and the one to re-read at 3 a.m.
6. **The skills, in steps:** [`health/care-techniques.md`](health/care-techniques.md) — holding (the four holds and how to still be holding him an hour later), the 5-step swaddle and its troubleshooting, diaper change including meconium/blowouts/fit leaks, burp, bath, nose (saline + suction), nails, taking his temperature the correct way, giving medicine, sling/carrier fit, car seat, tummy time. Read the section *before* the first attempt, not during it.
7. **The plan you actually run:** [`health/first-six-weeks-program.md`](health/first-six-weeks-program.md) — the 24-hour feed–burp–change–awake–sleep cycle, the shift roster that protects Ervina's 4-hour block of sleep, the week-by-week focus for weeks 1–6, the feeding phases, the pumping ramp for going back to work, and the checkpoints you don't move.
8. **Read once before you need it:** [`health/red-flags-and-emergencies.md`](health/red-flags-and-emergencies.md) — danger signs for Eldric *and* Ervina, choking/CPR, phone numbers. (≥38 °C under 3 months = hospital, immediately.) And for the sick day that *isn't* an emergency — the snuffly nose, the posset, the rash, the jab reaction: [`health/illness-care.md`](health/illness-care.md).
9. **Feeding:** [`food-and-feeding/breastfeeding.md`](food-and-feeding/breastfeeding.md) — latch, diaper-by-day gauge, problems, expressing/storage, who to call. If any bottle is in the picture — expressed milk, a top-up, or formula — read [`food-and-feeding/formula-and-bottles.md`](food-and-feeding/formula-and-bottles.md) **before** the first one: the ≥70 °C water rule and the exact-scoop rule are the two things a 3 a.m. mistake gets wrong.
10. **Ervina:** [`health/postpartum-recovery.md`](health/postpartum-recovery.md) — the stitch, bleeding, pelvic floor, the 4-hour sleep block, and the baby-blues-vs-postpartum-depression screen. Eric should read it too, so he knows what's normal and what to act on.
11. **The clock:** [`admin-and-money/42-day-paperwork-checklist.md`](admin-and-money/42-day-paperwork-checklist.md) — birth registration by **Mon 9 Nov 2026**, Special Pass expires **Mon 7 Dec 2026**, KBRI passport + Dependant's Pass chain.
12. **Appointments + records:** [`health/pd-visits-and-vaccinations.md`](health/pd-visits-and-vaccinations.md) — first check is the **Day 3–5 jaundice visit, Thu 29 – Sat 31 Oct 2026** — and [`health/newborn-screening-and-discharge.md`](health/newborn-screening-and-discharge.md) for what the hospital did to him (Vitamin K, BCG, hearing, G6PD) and the discharge checklist.
13. **When there's a calm hour:** [`development/first-three-months.md`](development/first-three-months.md), then the parenting guide, slowly.

Each folder has its own `README.md` explaining what belongs there, so new notes have an obvious home. Rule of thumb:

- `preparation/` = pre-birth planning, archived at the birth — don't run the newborn weeks out of it.
- `shopping/` = what we actually bought, with prices and links.
- `parenting/` = philosophy and approach; dated observations and stage logs go to `health/`, `food-and-feeding/`, or `development/`.
- `admin-and-money/` = deadlines, documents, and cash (the 42-day clock lives here — as a checklist, not just an explanation).

## How to use these documents

- **In the newborn weeks, use the "Start here" order above** — rules, skills, the program, emergencies, feeding, paperwork, appointments. That sequence is the job.
- **Day to day, run the program** ([`health/first-six-weeks-program.md`](health/first-six-weeks-program.md)) and keep the dos-and-donts page where anyone holding him can see it. Check the current week on Sunday night; log feeds and diapers every day.
- **Read the parenting guide slowly**, section by section, when there's a calm hour. It starts with a reality check and a foundation (secure attachment), then works through each concern, and ends with practical tools, an age-by-age plan, and a 15-point family charter.
- All documents are living notes: update them as plans, budgets, and the family's needs change — and date what actually happened (weights, vaccines, decisions) in the record files, so future-you isn't guessing.
