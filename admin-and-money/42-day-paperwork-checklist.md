# 42-Day Paperwork Checklist — Eldric

> **The clock started at birth: Mon 26 Oct 2026.** Eldric is deemed to hold a Special Pass for **42 days from birth — it expires Mon 7 Dec 2026.** Any unauthorised stay beyond that is an offence under the Immigration Act. This file is the tick-off version; the full explanation is [`../preparation/baby-preparation-shopping-list.md`](../preparation/baby-preparation-shopping-list.md) §10 and pregnancy [Ch. 9](https://github.com/eric-and-ervina/pregnancy/blob/main/course/09-singapore-admin.md).
> **Sponsor check (done):** the DP sponsor must be an EP/S Pass holder on **≥ $6,000 fixed monthly salary**. Eric's salary is **$6,500/month — he qualifies, with $500 of headroom.** That headroom is thin: any salary/pass change before the DP is issued goes to the top of the risk list. Ervina's S Pass and leave are the other moving part — if she stops working, her pass ends with the job and she moves to a DP under Eric (same rule); time any resignation deliberately.

## If it slips — the fallback (know it before you need it)

- **Passport late, Special Pass expiring within 7 days:** request an **extension of the Special Pass** at ICA / via MOM's newborn extension form — **Eldric must be present** — as soon as you see it coming, *not* on Day 42.
- **No DP route at all:** ICA Visitor Services Centre (Level 4) in person, with Eldric, before expiry.
- Escalation order if HR/employer stalls: Eric chases HR → HR contacts MOM newborn expedite → ICA extension as the safety net. A stalled DP at Day 35 with no extension filed is the failure mode; everything above exists to prevent exactly that.
